Acme Research Institute

Acme Research Institute was founded in 2018.

The institute's primary research areas are artificial intelligence,
machine learning, information retrieval, and natural language processing.

The institute has three research divisions:
1. Machine Learning
2. Information Retrieval
3. Natural Language Processing

The Information Retrieval division studies search systems,
retrieval-augmented generation, and document ranking.

The institute's main laboratory is located in Bengaluru.

The institute launched its RAG research program in 2024.
